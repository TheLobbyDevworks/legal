Privacy Policy

Effective October 19, 2026

In short: each product stores only what it needs to work, which is listed below. We never sell data or use it for advertising.

1. Our approach

  • Each product collects only the data it needs to do its job.
  • We never sell, rent or trade data, and we don't use it for advertising.
  • Our bots don't read or store your messages unless a product's section below says so and explains why.
  • When you use a slash command, Discord tells the bot who used it so it can check permissions. Unless a product's section says otherwise, that is used in the moment and not saved.

2. What each product stores

  • The Lobby Announcer
  • Lobby Service

The Lobby Announcer

Discord bot that posts game updates, anime episode alerts, redeem codes and news to chosen channels

DataWhy
Server (guild) IDTo know which server a setting belongs to
Channel and role IDs chosen by adminsTo post in the right channel and mention the right role
The games, shows and news feeds a server followsTo know what to check
IDs and dates of items already posted, and redeem codes seenTo avoid posting the same thing twice and to mark expired codes
Game or show requests: the member's user ID, what they asked for, any note they wrote, and how staff handled itTo show the request to staff and tell the member the result

Shared with: Anthropic receives the text of public announcements and articles so it can write short summaries. No server or member information is sent. Steam, HoYoLAB, Mojang, AniList, community code trackers and news sites are only read from; nothing about you is sent to them.

Deleting it: admins can remove a feed with the Remove button in /announce. When the bot is removed from a server, everything it stored for that server is deleted automatically. To delete your own requests, email us.

Lobby Service

Discord bot for private support tickets: questions, reports and problems

DataWhy
Server ID, and the ticket channel, transcript channel and staff roles chosen by adminsTo open tickets in the right place and add the right staff
For each ticket: the member's user ID, topic, the staff member who claimed and closed it, the close reason, timestamps and an optional 1 to 5 ratingTo run the ticket, limit open tickets per member and close quiet tickets
The messages written inside a ticketWhen a ticket closes, they are saved as a transcript file in a staff-only channel of that server, so staff can look back at what happened

Who can see it: a ticket is a private thread that only the member who opened it and the server's staff can see. Reports stay confidential: the reported member is never added to the ticket.

Shared with: no one. Ticket messages are not sent to any AI service or third party. Transcripts are stored inside the server's own Discord channel.

Deleting it: server staff can delete transcripts from their Discord channel at any time. When the bot is removed from a server, its ticket records for that server are deleted automatically. To delete your own ticket records, email us.

3. Services we rely on

  • Discord runs the platform our bots live on. Its Privacy Policy covers your use of Discord.
  • Anthropic provides AI features in some products. Only the content listed in that product's section is sent. See Anthropic's Privacy Policy.
  • Hosting providers run our bots and store the data listed above.
  • Other platforms a product reads from, such as Steam, are named in that product's section.

4. How long we keep it

We keep data only while a product needs it, as described in its section. When you remove one of our bots from your server, it deletes the data it stored for that server automatically. If it was offline at the time, this happens the next time it starts.

5. Security

Data is stored on private servers that only our team can access. Ticket transcripts are stored in the server's own Discord channel, where that server's staff control who can see them. No storage method is perfectly secure, so we keep the data we hold to a minimum.

6. Children

Our Services are meant for use on Discord, which has its own minimum age requirements. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what data we hold about you or your server, and to have it corrected or deleted. Email us and we'll respond within 30 days.

8. New products and changes

When we launch a new product or change what one collects, we add or update its section above and change the date at the top.
